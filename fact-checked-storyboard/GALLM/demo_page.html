From a patient question to a checked video prompt

Ask a patient question. The answer is built live: facts come from a knowledge graph of clinical guidelines, one model writes a line for each fact, and a different model checks every line against its own fact. Lines that fail are rewritten once or removed.

Part 1 · Steps 1–3 · Code only

What the knowledge graph returned

The question's words are matched to the graph's own concept names, two fixed SPARQL queries collect claims and recommendations that mention them, and the best facts (at most 10) are kept with their guideline and page.

Part 2 · Steps 4–7

The script, line by line

Each fact becomes one slot. The writer (gpt-oss-20b) turns it into one plain spoken line. Every line then passes code checks and a judge (gpt-oss-120b) that sees only that line's own fact.
